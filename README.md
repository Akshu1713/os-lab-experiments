## Lab Journey & Implementation (Experiment 10)
### 1. Pre-Production
- Collected the screenshots of Experiments 1 to 9 and wrote a narration script.
- Goal: a clear walkthrough video with a smooth zoom intro and consistent colour grading.

### 2. Applying Advanced Filters
- **Color Grading:** contrast, saturation and gamma adjusted.
- **Vignette** filter for a cinematic look.
- **Fade in / Fade out** between slides.

### 3. Keyframe Animation Workflow
- Enabled keyframes on the *Size, Position & Rotate* filter.
- Keyframe 1 at `00:00:00:12` (zoom 100%) and keyframe 2 at `00:00:03:12` (zoom 115%): smooth linear zoom-in on the title card.
